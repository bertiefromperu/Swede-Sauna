Sara's story

Why a sauna on a beach.

Sara's introduction is coming — she'll share the story behind Swede Sauna, her love of Robert's Cove, and her philosophy around heat therapy and wellbeing.

In the meantime: Swede Sauna is a genuinely one-of-a-kind experience in Ireland. A hand-crafted wood-fired barrel sauna at the waterline of one of West Cork's most beautiful hidden beaches. Sara runs every session herself.

Corporate partnerships

Wellness packages for teams.

Swede Sauna partners with multinationals — including Dell — offering bulk session packages for employee wellbeing programmes and team days. Pricing is flexible and tracked digitally, making it simple to roll out across teams.

For corporate enquiries, reach out to Sara directly via Instagram.
